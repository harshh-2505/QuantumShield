The threat

Harvest now, decrypt later

Traffic protected by RSA or elliptic-curve key exchange can be recorded today and decrypted once a quantum computer can run Shor's algorithm. Nobody has to break in later: the ciphertext is already in someone's archive.

That makes key exchange the urgent part. A signature only has to hold up while it is being verified, but recorded ciphertext has to stay secret for as long as the data matters. QuantumShield ranks findings that way, and migrates key exchange first.

Michele Mosca's rule of thumb: if the years your data must stay secret (x) plus the years your migration takes (y) exceed the years until a cryptographically relevant quantum computer (z), you are already late. Try your own numbers.

How it works

From unknown crypto to a measured migration

One command-line tool, five stages. Every cryptographic operation goes through OpenSSL 3.5; QuantumShield never implements ML-KEM or ML-DSA itself.

  1. 01

    Scan

    Probes TLS 1.0 to 1.3, every key-exchange group, cipher suites and mTLS. Parses nginx, Apache, HAProxy, Envoy, OpenSSL, SSH, PEM files and source code.

    qs scan -e pay=payments:443 -c ./etc/nginx
  2. 02

    Inventory

    Writes a CycloneDX 1.6 cryptographic bill of materials: algorithms, certificates, protocols and keys, checked against the official schema.

    → cbom.json · inventory.json
  3. 03

    Analyze

    About 30 rules assign a quantum status to every algorithm (broken, weakened, resistant, hybrid) and a risk score to every service, key exchange first.

    → findings · report.md
  4. 04

    Migrate

    A phased plan with patched configs for nginx, Apache, HAProxy, OpenSSL and SSH, or a hybrid mTLS sidecar for services you can't change.

    qs migrate inventory.json -o plan/
  5. 05

    Measure

    Handshake time, CPU and bytes, classical against hybrid, in-process and over the network. Then a re-scan proves the result.

    qs bench net -t pay=payments:8443

Quickstart

Run the whole demo in one command

Needs Docker. Builds the image (Python 3.15 on Debian trixie with OpenSSL 3.5), starts the three services, and runs every stage above in about a minute and a half.

The full demo

$ git clone https://github.com/harshh-2505/quantumshield
$ cd quantumshield/demo
$ ./run-demo.sh        # scan, CBOM, plan, sidecars, benchmarks
$ open out/dashboard.html
$ ./run-demo.sh down   # clean up

On your own systems

$ qs scan -e api=api.example.com:443 -c /etc/nginx -o out
$ qs migrate out/inventory.json -o out/plan
$ qs pki init --alg mldsa65 && qs pki issue api
$ qs proxy --mode ingress --listen 0.0.0.0:8443 \
    --upstream 127.0.0.1:8080 --cert pki/api.crt \
    --key pki/api.key --ca pki/ca.crt --pq-auth
